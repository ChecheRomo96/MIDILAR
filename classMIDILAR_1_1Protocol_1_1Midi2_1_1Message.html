Detailed Description

Native MIDI 2.0 channel-voice message, without UMP routing (SPEC-NS-2..3).

Two inline field words store the seven supported channel-voice families: notes, poly pressure, CC, program/bank, channel pressure and pitch bend. No UMP message-type or group is stored. SysEx, system, utility, flex and stream messages belong to their own families, not to this class. Invalid factories return one canonical invalid message; no allocation.
