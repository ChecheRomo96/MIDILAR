Detailed Description

Portable MIDI protocol, parsing and device processing built on Foundation and MCC.

MIDILAR uses the MIDILAR namespace and consumes the stable CMake targets Foundation::Foundation and MCC::MCC. Applications may include MIDILAR.h for the complete enabled surface or a documented module/component header for narrow access.
