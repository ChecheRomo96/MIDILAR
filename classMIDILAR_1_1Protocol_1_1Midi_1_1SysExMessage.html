Detailed Description

Non-owning complete F0..F7 message in caller storage (SPEC-M1-3).

Copies share the same bytes. Keep the caller buffer alive and unchanged while reading the view. Build validates everything before writing, supports overlapping payloads, and never allocates or truncates.
