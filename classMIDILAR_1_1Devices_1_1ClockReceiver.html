Detailed Description

Follows incoming MIDI clock: transport state, song position and tempo (SPEC-CLK-1..3).

Every packet passes through unchanged. Start resets Position() to zero, Continue resumes, Stop pauses, Song Position moves it while stopped, and each timing clock advances it while running. The tempo is measured from the time between timing clocks, smoothed over about eight clocks.
