Song Position Pointer message (0xF2)

The Song Position Pointer message specifies a position within a song as a 14-bit value. It is used in MIDI clock synchronization to allow devices to continue playback from a specific position. One unit is six MIDI clocks. Position 16 is 96 clocks, or four quarter notes at 24 PPQN.

  • Data Byte 1: Least significant 7 bits of the 14-bit position.
  • Data Byte 2: Most significant 7 bits of the 14-bit position.
