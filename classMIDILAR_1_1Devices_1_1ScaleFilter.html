Detailed Description

Keeps notes on an MCC::Scale, compared by pitch class (MCC::Scale::ContainsPitchClass).

Applies to every packet with a note (Packet::HasNote()); other packets pass unchanged, and so does everything while the scale is invalid. A note that would move outside [0, 127] is dropped. Held notes keep the mapping they started with until their Note Off (HeldNotes).
