Detailed Description

Measures how far the local Foundation::Time::Clock is from a reference MIDI clock of known tempo, in parts per million (SPEC-CLK-4).

Feed it a reference clock, for example a DAW at 120 BPM, and call Start() with that tempo. After clocks timing clocks it reports the correction to apply to the local clock frequency: a local oscillator that runs 500 ppm slow gives -500, and the corrected frequency is the nominal one times (1 + ppm / 1e6) (for a 1 MHz micros() clock, 1000000 + ppm). Longer measurements average out transport jitter. Every packet passes through.
