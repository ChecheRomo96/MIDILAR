Drop-frame numbering represents 30000/1001 frames per second. It skips numbers 0 and 1 at each minute except multiples of ten; no video frames are discarded. TimeCode::From rejects the omitted numbers. The rate occupies Full Frame hour-byte bits 6-5 and quarter-frame piece 7 nibble bits 2-1.
