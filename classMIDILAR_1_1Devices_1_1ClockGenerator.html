Detailed Description

Sends MIDI clock at a tempo, timed by a Foundation::Time::Clock (SPEC-CLK-1..3).

Call Update() often (every loop iteration); it emits every timing clock that is due. Clocks are scheduled from the previous due time, not from the call time, so late calls do not make the tempo drift. Timing clocks run while stopped too, so receivers keep the tempo; Position() only advances while running.

The tempo is in hundredths of a BPM (12000 is 120 BPM).
